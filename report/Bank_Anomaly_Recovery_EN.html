Part 1 · Business Objective

1. Why this analysis

A bank / payment provider processes 1.05M personal transactions daily; the core risk is money lost to fraud, anomaly or operational error (revenue protection). Two business questions:

  • Anomaly detection (fund protection): which transactions are abnormal — over account balance (₹ insufficient but a transaction happened, 9.9%), late-night large (4.5%), extreme amounts (max ₹1.56M vs median ₹459). Unsupervised methods put suspicious ones into a review queue.
  • Customer fund-value tiering: top 25% of customers drive 81% of volume — high-value accounts need differentiated service, low-value use low-cost touch.

One-line goal: use explainable anomaly detection to flag "must-review" transactions (plug fund leakage), and customer-value tiering to decide "where resources go".

Honest boundary. This data has ~1.19 transactions per customer on average, and balance vs cumulative volume are uncorrelated (r=0.06) → a true balance reconciliation is not possible (not multiple lines per same account). This project is positioned as transaction-level anomaly detection; it does not fake "account reconciliation", and does not claim fraud labels (no labels → unsupervised, see Part 4).

Part 4 · Anomaly Modeling

4. Unsupervised anomaly detection (principle · why · how)

4.1 Why unsupervised (no fraud labels)

This dataset has no "fraud/anomaly" label, so supervised training is impossible. We use unsupervised anomaly detection: starting from "most transactions are normal", find the points that deviate. This matches real payment-risk practice — anomalies are rare and hard to label; first unsupervised discovery, then human review accumulates labels and gradually moves to supervised.

4.2 Three methods: principle · why chosen · how used

MethodPrincipleWhyHow
MAD / robust z-scoreRobust standardization by median & MAD, >3.5 flagged outlierMore robust than mean on long tail, most explainable, zero costSingle-dimension amount baseline
Isolation ForestRandomly split feature space; anomalies are "easy to isolate" (short path) → high scoreMulti-dim (amount+time+balance) combos; O(n) scales to 1.05M linesMain method: score, top1% into review queue
Business rulesDomain rules (p99 large & over-balance/late-night)Directly explainable, implementable as real-time rulesRisk system instant block/alert

Why not deep learning. 1.05M transaction lines could train an autoencoder, but: ① no labels to evaluate supervised; ② anomaly detection must answer "why this line was flagged" (compliance & CS), tree-ensemble/density methods are naturally explainable; ③ Isolation Forest is already efficient at this scale. DL is less explainable with limited gain, so it is not used — consistent with "use only when the business needs it, explain the principle".

Part 5 · Recommendations

5. One actionable plan

Fund protection

1 · Real-time over-balance blocking

9.9% of lines over-balance (₹346M). Check balance pre-transaction; trigger check/review on over-balance; prioritize late-night over-balance large amounts.

Review queue

2 · Multi-signal review tickets

Isolation Forest + rules double-hit (370) reviewed first, Top50 reviewed same-day; rules single-hit next — coverage and explainability together.

High-value ops

3 · L1 high-value customers

top25% drive 81% of volume. Dedicated CS, fast large-review lane, targeted limits; L4 with low-cost automated touch.

Model evolution

4 · Review → labels → supervised

Turn review results into fraud/anomaly labels; once enough, train a supervised classifier (XGBoost) for higher precision and measure AUC.
